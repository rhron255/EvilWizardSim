## The design

`Artifact.defense: number` and `Artifact.effect: string` both go. One field
replaces them:

```ts
Artifact.power: ArtifactPower   // required, exactly one
```

`effect` is DERIVED from `power` by one renderer, so the prose can never
disagree with the number (failure mode 4 is a hand-written mirror of exactly
this shape). Six power kinds, each a modifier of a system that already exists
— no new era-end tick, so no new disclosure channel is invented:

| power | what it does | lever | disclosed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wards` | Defense +v | `defenseOf` | the wards readout's Relics term |
| `vigil` | hero threat rises v less each decline era | `threatGainFor` | `his threat +N an era` |
| `undimmed` | notoriety decays v less each decline era | `decayFor` | the relic card only — the erosion is deliberately quiet (rule 5) |
| `discipline` | loyalty drifts v less each decline era | drift in `run.ts` | the `loyaltyDrift` systemic row |
| `haggle` | every follower cost is v smaller | `applyEffects` | `projectEffects` prints the reduced cost |
| `grace` | every standing LOSS is v smaller | `applyStanding` | `projectEffects` prints the softened delta |

`haggle`/`grace` need no new disclosure at all: they change what `applyEffects`
does, and `projectEffects` IS `applyEffects` against a draft, so the offer card
prints the real number for free.

## One structural note worth carrying forward

A quality review made the strongest criticism of this sprint and it is worth
recording rather than burying: the 29 authored `requires` gates restate what
the validator's `tradeOf` already DERIVES from each option's own effects —
which is the inverse of this sprint's own flagship move (deleting
`Artifact.effect` because there should be no second place to say it). Issue
#61's branch already carries `impliedGatesOf`/`isOptionPickable`, so the two
approaches will collide on merge.

It was not acted on here, deliberately: it is a re-architecture of the offer
pool with real balance blast radius, and #61 itself records that the last
attempt regressed the game. It belongs to #61.
